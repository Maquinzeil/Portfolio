WHAT HAPPENS DURING THIS STAGE

Content Preparation

Final content is organized, formatted, and prepared for publishing across the agreed social media platforms.

Scheduling

Posts can be scheduled according to the agreed content calendar and posting schedule.

Content Organization

I help keep content files, ideas, captions, and schedules organized for a smoother workflow.

Engagement Support

Depending on the service package, I can help monitor comments and messages, respond to basic interactions, and flag important inquiries.

Ongoing Support

As your business changes, content can adapt to new promotions, products, services, events, seasonal campaigns, and updated goals.

ENGAGEMENT SUPPORT MAY INCLUDE

  • Checking and responding to comments
  • Monitoring messages
  • Flagging important inquiries
  • Keeping track of engagement activity

KEEPING THINGS ORGANIZED

  • Content files and asset organization
  • Caption and idea tracking
  • Content calendar management
  • Clear scheduling and workflow support

THE GOAL

To help your social media stay organized, consistent, and easier to manage, so you can focus more time on running your business.
